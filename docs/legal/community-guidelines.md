# NoZar — Community Guidelines

**Effective Date:** [Launch Date]

These guidelines help keep NoZar safe and fair for everyone. Violating them may result in warnings, suspension, or permanent removal from the platform.

---

## Be Honest

- **List items and services accurately.** Describe any flaws, damage, or limitations. Use real, recent photos — not stock images or photos from the internet.
- **Set realistic Estimated Values.** Don't inflate values to manipulate trades or make your offering appear more valuable than it is.
- **Represent yourself truthfully.** Your profile information, skills, and qualifications should be accurate.

## Be Respectful

- **Respond to trade inquiries within 48 hours** or decline them. Leaving people hanging wastes everyone's time.
- **Honour Trade Agreements.** Cancellation after contact information has been exchanged will be recorded on your profile and affects your completion rate.
- **Communicate clearly and professionally.** Disagreements happen — handle them with respect.
- **Meet at safe, public locations.** Never pressure someone to meet at a private location or come to your home.

## Stay Safe

- **Don't share contact details outside the Trade Agreement system.** The platform protects you by controlling when and how contact info is exchanged.
- **Report suspicious accounts or messages immediately.** If something feels wrong, it probably is.
- **Trust your instincts.** If a trade offer seems too good to be true, or a person makes you uncomfortable, cancel the trade. Your safety comes first.
- **Use the suggested safe meetup locations** whenever possible.

## Don't Abuse the Platform

- **No spam or duplicate listings.** One listing per item or service.
- **No commercial advertising disguised as barter listings.** The platform is for genuine trading, not marketing.
- **No fake accounts.** One account per person. Creating multiple accounts to bypass limits is a bannable offense.
- **No harvesting** other users' contact information, photos, or personal data.
- **No discriminatory behaviour.** All users are welcome regardless of race, gender, religion, sexual orientation, or disability.

## What You Can't List

See the full [Prohibited Items list](/legal/terms-of-service#prohibited-items--services) in our Terms of Service. In summary: nothing illegal, dangerous, or harmful.

## Consequences

| Violation | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| 1st minor violation | Warning |
| 2nd minor violation | 7-day suspension |
| 3rd minor violation | Permanent ban |
| Severe violation (fraud, threats, prohibited items, harassment) | Immediate permanent ban |

We review reports within 48 hours. If you believe an action was taken in error, email community@bartersa.co.za to appeal.

## Reporting

If you encounter a violation, use the **Report** button available on every profile, listing, and chat message. Reports are confidential — the reported user will not know who reported them.

---

*These guidelines exist to keep everyone safe. If you're acting in good faith and treating people the way you'd want to be treated, you have nothing to worry about.*
