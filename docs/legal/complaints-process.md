## NoZar — Complaints & Dispute Resolution Process

**Effective Date:** 13 April 2026
**Last Updated:** 13 April 2026

## 1. Introduction

At NoZar, we take every complaint seriously. We understand that things don't always go smoothly — whether it's a trade that didn't work out, a concern about another user, or an issue with the Platform itself. We're committed to handling your complaints fairly, transparently, and as quickly as possible.

This document sets out how to lodge a complaint, what happens after you do, and where to turn if you feel your complaint hasn't been resolved to your satisfaction. This process complies with the Consumer Protection Act 68 of 2008 ("CPA") and the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 ("POPIA").

## 2. Scope

This complaints process covers:

- **Trade disputes** — disagreements between Users about a Barter Transaction (item condition, non-delivery, misrepresentation).
- **Platform issues** — bugs, downtime, feature malfunctions, or errors affecting your experience.
- **Safety concerns** — reports of unsafe behaviour, harassment, or threats during meetups or in-app communication.
- **Account issues** — incorrect suspensions, verification problems, account access difficulties.
- **Billing & subscriptions** — payment errors, subscription disputes, boost token issues.
- **Privacy & data** — concerns about how your personal information is collected, used, or shared (POPIA complaints).
- **Content & listings** — disputes over removed listings, moderation decisions, or intellectual property claims.

## 3. How to File a Complaint

You can lodge a complaint through any of the following channels:

### 3.1. In-App (Recommended)

Navigate to **Settings → Help → File a Complaint**. This is the fastest way to reach us — your account details are automatically attached, and you can upload evidence directly.

### 3.2. Email

Send your complaint to **complaints@bartersa.co.za**. We monitor this inbox during business hours (Monday to Friday, 08:00–17:00 SAST).

### 3.3. What to Include

To help us investigate your complaint efficiently, please provide:

- **Your account details** — your username or the email address linked to your account.
- **A clear description** — what happened, when it happened, and who was involved.
- **Trade ID** (if applicable) — found in your trade history under the specific transaction.
- **Supporting evidence** — screenshots, photos of items received, chat logs, or any other relevant documentation.
- **Desired outcome** — let us know what resolution you're hoping for.

Anonymous complaints are accepted, but please note that this may limit our ability to investigate fully or provide you with a personal response.

## 4. Our Complaint Handling Process

### Step 1: Acknowledgment (within 48 hours)

We will acknowledge receipt of your complaint within **48 hours** (excluding weekends and public holidays). You'll receive a reference number to track the progress of your case.

### Step 2: Investigation (5–10 business days)

A member of our support team will review your complaint. Depending on the nature of the issue, this may involve:

- Reviewing trade history, chat logs, and platform data.
- Contacting the other party involved (for trade disputes).
- Consulting with our technical, safety, or legal teams.
- Requesting additional information from you.

If we need more time, we'll let you know and provide an updated timeline.

### Step 3: Resolution

Once our investigation is complete, we'll notify you of the outcome. This notification will include:

- A summary of our findings.
- The action we've taken (or why no action was taken).
- Any remedies or next steps available to you.

### Step 4: Appeal

If you're not satisfied with the outcome, you may request an **appeal** within **14 calendar days** of receiving the resolution. Appeals are escalated to a senior member of our team who was not involved in the original investigation.

We will respond to your appeal within **10 business days**.

## 5. Trade Dispute Resolution

Trade disputes between Users follow a structured mediation process:

### 5.1. Internal Mediation

When a trade dispute is raised, we'll facilitate communication between both parties and attempt to reach a fair resolution. Both parties will have the opportunity to present their side.

### 5.2. Platform Review

If mediation does not resolve the dispute, our team will conduct an independent review. We may consider:

- The original Listing description, photos, and Estimated Value.
- Chat logs between the parties.
- Trade agreement details and confirmation records.
- User ratings and trade history.
- Any evidence submitted by either party.

### 5.3. Possible Outcomes

After review, we may take one or more of the following actions:

- **Trade reversal** — where both parties are instructed to return exchanged items, and the trade is marked as reversed on both profiles.
- **Account action** — warnings, temporary suspensions, or permanent bans for Users found to have acted in bad faith.
- **Rating adjustment** — removal or amendment of unfair ratings linked to the disputed trade.
- **No action** — where evidence is inconclusive or both parties bear shared responsibility.

### 5.4. Timeline

We aim to resolve all trade disputes within **30 calendar days** of the complaint being filed. Complex cases involving multiple parties or external verification may take longer — we'll keep you informed throughout.

## 8. Record Keeping

8.1. All complaints are logged in our internal complaint management system, including the date received, nature of the complaint, actions taken, and outcome.

8.2. Complaint records are retained for a minimum of **3 years** from the date of resolution, in compliance with the CPA.

8.3. These records may be used to identify patterns, improve our Platform, and demonstrate compliance with regulatory requirements.

8.4. Anonymous complaints are logged and investigated where possible, but our ability to follow up may be limited without your contact details.
