## Account & Login

### "Account already exists" error

If you see "Account already exists" when trying to register, it means you already have an account with that email. Use the **Forgot Password** link on the login page to reset your password and sign in. If you don't remember which email you used, try each one you might have used — the system will tell you if one works.

### I can't log in

1. Go to **nozar.co.za/forgot-password**
2. Enter your email address
3. Check your inbox (and spam folder) for a password reset link
4. Click the link and set a new password
5. Sign in with your new password

If you're still stuck, contact NoZar support and they can manually verify your account.

### I'm stuck on the "verify your email" screen

Scroll all the way down that page — there's a **"Wrong email? Sign out"** button at the bottom. Tap it to sign out and start fresh, or sign in with a different email.

### I can't sign out

The sign out button is in your **Dashboard → Profile → Account** tab. Scroll down to the bottom of the account settings page.

### Wrong email on my account

Once signed in:
1. Go to **Profile → Account** tab
2. You'll see your current email with a **"Change"** button next to it
3. Tap Change, enter the correct email + your password
4. A verification link will be sent to the new address

If you can't sign in because the email is wrong, use **Forgot Password** and try different emails until you find the one registered. If that fails, contact support.

### My email verification isn't arriving

1. Check your spam/promotions folder
2. Wait a few minutes — it can take up to 5 minutes
3. If you're on the verify-email page, try clicking "Resend" if available
4. If nothing works, contact support to manually verify your account

### Phone OTP (verification code) isn't arriving

1. Make sure you entered your number with the correct format: **+27XXXXXXXXX** (e.g., +27821234567)
2. Check your SMS inbox — it comes as a regular text message
3. Wait up to 2 minutes
4. If you still don't receive it, the SMS service may be temporarily down. Contact NoZar support.

### Device verification failed (stuck at D-07)

This happens when your device fingerprint doesn't match what's on record. This is a security feature. Contact NoZar support to have your device fingerprint cleared.

## Profile & Settings

### How do I edit my profile?

From the dashboard:
1. Tap **Profile** in the sidebar/bottom nav
2. You can edit your display name, bio, location, and profile picture
3. Tap **Save** when done

### How do I change my email?

Go to **Profile → Account** tab. You'll see your email with a **"Change"** button next to it. Enter your new email and password.

### How do I change my password?

Go to **Profile → Account** tab. There's a **"Change Password"** section where you can update it.

### How do I delete my account?

Go to **Profile → Account** tab. Scroll to the bottom — there's a **"Delete Account"** option. Note: this is permanent and cannot be undone.

## Listings & Trading

### How do I create a listing?

1. Go to your Dashboard
2. Tap the **"Add item"** button (green button in sidebar or bottom nav)
3. Add photos, a title, description, category, and condition
4. Tap **Submit** — your listing will appear on the map and feed

### How do I edit or delete a listing?

Go to your listing by tapping it on the map or in your profile. You'll see **Edit** and **Delete** buttons. Only the listing owner can edit or delete.

### How do I ping/message someone about their listing?

1. Find the listing on the map or feed
2. Tap the listing to open it
3. Tap the **"Ping"** button to express interest
4. The other person can accept your ping, which opens a chat
5. Once connected, you can discuss the exchange

### What is trading/pinging?

When you see a listing you're interested in, you **ping** the owner. If they're interested too, you're connected in a chat where you can arrange the exchange. NoZar doesn't handle payments or shipping — you arrange that directly with the other person.

### How does the trust system work?

Users build trust through successful trades. After a trade, users can leave feedback. A user's trust score (displayed as friendly, cautious, etc.) shows how reliable they are in the community.

## Subscription & Billing

### What subscription plans are available?

- **Free (Beta Promo):** Full access during the beta period (90 days)
- After the beta, subscription fees apply for continued access

### How do I manage my subscription?

Go to **Dashboard → Billing** to see your current plan, payment history, and manage your subscription.

### How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to **Dashboard → Billing** and look for the cancel option. Your access will continue until the end of the current billing period.

### My payment failed

If your payment fails:
1. Check your payment method is valid and has sufficient funds
2. Go to **Dashboard → Billing** to update your payment details
3. The system will automatically retry after a few days
4. If it continues to fail, your account may be downgraded

## Technical & Safety

### Is NoZar a marketplace?

No. NoZar is a **membership-based community platform** where members can list and discover items and services. NoZar does not handle payments between members, take commissions, or process transactions. All exchanges are arranged directly between members.

### How does NoZar verify users?

Users must verify their email address to use the platform. Phone verification adds an extra layer of security. The system also uses device fingerprinting to prevent fraud.

### My location isn't working

If the map or location features aren't working:
1. Make sure you've granted location permissions in your browser
2. Try refreshing the page
3. On mobile, check that location services are enabled
4. If using a desktop browser, you may need to connect to the internet

### I'm seeing an error I don't understand

Most errors display a helpful message. If you're seeing a technical error code (like D-07, E-03), screenshot it and contact support with the code.

## General

### Who can use NoZar?

NoZar is available in South Africa. You must be 18+ to use the platform.

### Is NoZar safe?

NoZar uses email verification, phone OTP, and device fingerprinting to secure accounts. However, since all exchanges happen between members directly, use common sense:
- Meet in public places for exchanges
- Don't share financial information
- Report suspicious users
- Check the other person's trust score before trading

### How do I contact NoZar support?

For issues you can't solve yourself, reach out to NoZar on WhatsApp or email. Support can look up your account, resend verifications, change your email, and fix technical issues that are blocking you.
