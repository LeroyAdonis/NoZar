# Product

## Register

product

## Users
South Africans trading goods, skills, and services without cash, especially everyday consumers trying to unlock value from what they already own or can do. They use NoZar in practical, real-world contexts: checking nearby listings, comparing value bands, starting pings, and arranging safe handovers with enough confidence to trade with a stranger. The system should leave room for a later expansion toward small businesses and verified enterprise barter once the business model is locked down.

## Product Purpose
NoZar is a barter platform that helps people swap directly instead of spending money. The product exists to make exchange feel credible, local, and useful: users can list assets, browse nearby matches, negotiate through pings, and complete a trust-aware handshake flow with clear value parity and verification signals. It should solve for consumer barter first without boxing the platform out of a future business-to-business layer.

## Brand Personality
Streetwise, trustworthy, energetic. The voice should feel local, direct, and confident, with enough edge to avoid generic fintech polish while still making safety, fairness, and legitimacy feel non-negotiable.

## Anti-references
Do not drift into generic fintech SaaS dashboards. Avoid looking sterile, overly corporate, or like a glossy "earn rewards" app that hides the real-world friction and trust requirements of barter. Secondary anti-patterns to watch for are luxury resale minimalism and soft pastel consumer marketplace styling.

## Design Principles
1. Trust must be visible before it is explained.
2. Real-world trade should feel active, local, and grounded in South African context.
3. Value exchange should feel fair and legible, not abstract or gamified.
4. The interface should project confidence through strong hierarchy and decisive copy.
5. Safety, verification, and deal state should always be easier to scan than promotional content.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Default to strong contrast, large tap targets, and clear status messaging on mobile-first screens. Support reduced-motion fallbacks where animation is decorative, keep labels and negotiation states explicit, and preserve readability for users navigating in bright outdoor light as well as dim indoor environments.
