Design Section 2 of 7 — Welcome Overlay (First Login)

Shows once on first login. Two sentences. Two buttons. Never appears again.

What the user sees

🔄
Trade without spending money
Swap your stuff, skills, and services directly with people nearby. No cash needed — just agree on a fair trade.
List my first item
Browse what's available first
🔒 Safe handover process  ·  Verified users

How it behaves

When it appears

First login only. A hasSeenWelcome flag is saved to the user's profile. Never shown again after dismissal.

Primary button

"List my first item" → opens the new 2-step listing wizard. Flag is marked seen.

Secondary button

"Browse what's available first" → dismisses overlay, lands on home feed. Flag is marked seen.

If they browse without listing

A quiet nudge strip stays at the top of the home feed: "Nothing listed yet — add your first item".
