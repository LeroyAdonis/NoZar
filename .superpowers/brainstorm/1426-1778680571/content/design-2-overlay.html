Design Section 2 of 7 — Welcome Overlay (First Login)

Shows once on first login. Two sentences. Two buttons. Never appears again.

First login — full screen
🔄
Trade without spending money
Swap your stuff, skills, and services directly with people nearby. No cash needed — just agree on a fair trade.
List my first item
Browse what's available first
🔒 Safe handover process · Verified users

Behaviour spec

When it appears

Only on first login, once the session is established. A hasSeenWelcome flag is stored in the user's profile after dismissal.

Primary button

"List my first item" → navigates to the 2-step listing wizard. Marks the flag as seen.

Secondary button

"Browse what's available first" → dismisses the overlay, lands on the home feed. Marks the flag as seen.

Trust line at the bottom

One line: "Safe handover process · Verified users". Addresses the first concern a stranger-to-stranger trade raises, before the user even asks.

After dismissal

If they chose "browse first" and still have no listings, the home feed shows a persistent but unobtrusive nudge strip at the top: "Nothing listed yet — add your first item".
