# NoZar Support Knowledge Base

## Account

### How to reset password
Go to the login page, tap "Forgot Password", enter your registered email or phone number, check your inbox/SMS for the reset link or OTP, then follow the instructions to set a new password.

### How to change email
Go to Profile → Settings → Email. Enter your new email and verify it. You'll receive a confirmation link.

### How to change phone number
Go to Profile → Settings → Phone. Enter your new number and verify with the OTP sent via SMS.

### Account verification
You need to verify your email and phone number to access all features. Check your inbox for the verification email and your SMS for the phone OTP.

## Trading

### How trading works
NoZar is a barter platform — you trade items and services without money. List what you have, browse what others offer, and arrange a trade through chat.

### How to list an item
Tap the "+" button → take or upload a photo → add a title and description → choose a category → set your trade preferences → publish.

### How to accept a trade
When someone expresses interest, chat with them to agree on terms. Both parties mark the trade as complete after exchanging items.

### Cancelling a trade
You can cancel a trade through the trade screen if both parties agree. If there's a dispute, contact support.

## Membership

### Membership tiers
NoZar has a free tier and paid Plus membership with extra features like priority listings and advanced matching.

### How to upgrade
Go to Profile → Billing → choose your plan → complete payment via PayFast.

### Free vs Plus
Free: basic listings, standard matching. Plus: priority listings, AI matching, advanced analytics, priority support.

## Safety

### Meeting for trades
Always meet in a public place during daylight hours. NoZar recommends police station parking lots or shopping malls. Never share your home address.

### Scam warnings
Never pay upfront fees, courier fees, or deposits. NoZar is a barter platform — no money should change hands between users for the items themselves. Report suspicious behaviour immediately.

### Reporting a user
Go to their profile → tap the three dots → "Report User". Or contact support with their username and details.

## Technical

### App not loading
Try closing and reopening the app. If that doesn't work, clear your browser/app cache or try a different browser.

### Push notifications not working
Check your device notification settings. Ensure NoZar has notification permission enabled. On iOS: Settings → NoZar → Notifications. On Android: Settings → Apps → NoZar → Notifications.

### Location not working
Ensure location services are enabled for NoZar in your device settings. NoZar uses location to show nearby listings and safe meetup spots.
